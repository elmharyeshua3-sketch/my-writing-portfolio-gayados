My Short Story

Knights

In a magical world, there was a kingdom that was unstoppable because of its three powerful knights who were raised and trained together. In their years, they conquered, destroyed, and won over many enemies. One day they arrived in a kingdom where the gods adore most. Chiru, the strongest of the three, rushed and attacked the kingdom with their army along with Dio, the youngest and most reckless, while Elthys stood behind. The three of them angered the gods. After the war, they were sailing back home. Then the god of the sea destroyed their ship. The three gods separated. Chiru and Dio were missing. Then Elthys woke up on an unknown island. When he woke up, he saw goblins dragging him deep inside a forest. He instantly fought back and struggled to take them down. In that forest, it was almost very dark. The big old trees are covering the sun from hitting the ground. It was so silent you can only hear the wind passing through and the birds chirping. As Elthys was exploring the forest, finding food and water to restore its energy, the gods sent a big serpent at him. Elthys was going more and more deeper in the forest. Then the big serpent came behind him. As it was about to open its mouth, Elthys sensed it, then quickly avoided the serpent. He fought it back quickly, severing its head off. After killing the serpent, Elthys sat on the grass and laid his back at a tree. 8 years had passed, almost everyday and night more beasts came at him, giving him scars and bruises. Then one night he broke down, and giving up he called out the gods, apologizing for what they have done. He shouted not because he was angry, but because he was tired. For 8 years he had been trying to build a boat, but beasts always came sent by the gods to destroy it. He was so desperate to go home, but the gods laughed at him watching him suffer. Then they heard him completely breaking and apologizing to them for what they have done. He and his brothers have done. He was just doing what his brothers wants, and what he thinks is good for their kingdom. He wonders why he is getting punished when he just follows orders. The gods heard him getting becoming guilty, thinking they have punished the wrong guy. So they all gathered then talked about what they should do. They talked about giving him a boat and blessing him as an apology. Elthys blacked out from exhaustion. By the time he woke up, he saw a boat, and his body feels new. He thanked the gods, then sailed home.
