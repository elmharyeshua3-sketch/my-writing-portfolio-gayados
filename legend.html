My Legend

Legend of Curses

There was a village on the top of a high mountain called Mystic. The wind there is very strong, it was so cold that every mortal being needed to wear thick jackets. At one of the houses, a woman named Frelin was giving birth. By her side there was a man named Ivar. Ivar is holding Frelin's hand very tight, not wanting to let her go while she is giving her best to give birth to their child. After a few hours, Ivar is holding their baby, and Frelin is lying on their bed. Ivar looks at the baby disappointed, while Frelin, as a mother of their baby, is weeping because their baby has a very little/short small legs. After Frelin went to sleep, Ivar took the baby down to the mountain. He was crying and talking to their baby, explaining himself why he was doing what he was thinking. He wanted to kill the child because he knows that the child won't survive in their village. At the bottom of the mountain, he finds a cave, then he enters it. The loud cry of the baby echoes, and Ivar lays his baby down, apologizing on what he's about to do. He picks up a rock, then raises it high as he can, aiming for the baby. A loud roar echoes from the cave deep inside the cave, making him stop. He dropped the rock, then ran nonstop, terrified of the sound of the roar. It sounded like it came from a big creature. After climbing the high mountain, he finds Frelin outside their house crying. She lunged at her husband, screaming, asking where their baby is. Her eyes are very red, and her tears streaming down her face nonstop. Ivar just stands there, not knowing what to say, while Frelin is pounding his body, yelling because she didn't have the strength to do anything else. She was so exhausted from crying and giving birth to their baby. Frelin keeps insisting of Ivar, begging him to bring her baby back. After Ivar looks at her, then tells her what he had done, Frelin screams like crazy again and tells Ivar that the gods will be angry for what he did. At the cave, the baby's cry is echoing, waking a god that was living there. The god wakes up with a big yawn that sounded like a roar; it echoed in the cave, scaring Ivar out. After Ivar ran, the god went to check what was going on. He only finds a baby with a rock beside him. He also noticed the baby's legs are so fragile. After a few moments, he lifts the baby, then takes him deep inside the cave. As he was taking care of it, he heard the cry of the baby's mother, and pitied the baby.
