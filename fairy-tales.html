My Fairy Tales

The Blood the Phoenix

Once upon a time, there was a boy frightened of the outside world. Whenever he goes out of their house, his chest tightens. He feels like everyone is watching him. It was a struggle for him to go to school every morning. At their school he was bullied because of his movements. He seemed so awkward, and he always stutters when someone talks to him. One night he found an old illustration made from his ancestors. He opens it to find a family of knights. He was so amazed at what he saw. He couldn't believe that he had ancestors like that. Years had passed, he is now 16 years old, and is still struggling with the same problems he had. One day when he was walking home, a beggar whispers his name behind him. "Drago." He looked behind him. The beggar behind him asked, "What do you want?" The beggar smiled, then became a big bird about the size of a human with shining feathers that looks like a fire. Drago passed out from what he had seen. At the time he woke up, he realizes that he is in a shining armor and looks around him, then sees fairies and a big kingdom in front of him. He asked the fairies where he is, and they answered he is in the kingdom of Elsian, the most peaceful of the three kingdoms. He walked down the mountain, he woke up, then headed straight to the castle. For the first time he didn't hesitate to walk through the crowds. While Drago was walking through the alleys, he notices that the people who live there know magic. The other people treated him with respect because of the armor he wears. Drago slowly opens his eyes and notices he is in a different place. He tries to move his body, and he felt so light. He looks around and notices that he is in a shady room where the only light is the candle beside his bed. He stood up and opens the wooden door, and there he saw a long red carpet, with armored knights walking past him. He wonders where he is and asks the knights where about his whereabouts. Then the guards told him that he is in the land of Elsian. The knights laughed and continued walking. Drago stands there completely shocked. He realized that he had dreamt about this magical world. He went outside the large castle, asking himself why the bird had taken him in this place. Outside the castle, he explores the whole kingdom. While exploring, he notices everyone has extraordinary abilities that no human can do in their world. The people in the land of Elsian can control water, fire, earth, or wind. Everyone there can control one of the elements. Drago was so mesmerized by what he was seeing, and continued exploring the town till the sun is setting. He looks around then realizes it was getting dark. Looking at the setting sun, he notices a flaming bird. He thinks it is the one that went inside his chest, but as he was about to follow it, he heard a voice in his head telling him not to. In front of the castle door, the guards asked him if he is the one that the phoenix had chosen. Drago doesn't know what they are talking about, till one of them pulls out a knife and scratches Drago's hand. Drago pulls his hand, then notices his blood looks like gold. They let Drago inside the castle and the king already prepared a feast for him. The king named Evren greeted him, and Drago smiles awkwardly. The king tells Drago that he is the last knight of their bloodline, and tells him to eat. After they ate, Evren kneels in front of Drago and tells him to save his daughter from the kingdom's enemy called Vreilla, where most corrupted creatures live, and they are ruled by Ragno, the most evil of them all. Drago stands there thinking he can't do it. The king encourages him, telling Drago that he is a knight with the blood of the phoenix. Then a few moments later it struck Drago. He realizes that he is tired of being always frightened. His mind is starting to think like a brave knight like his ancestors. After agreeing to the king, he prepared himself physically and mentally. Then he sat on a horse, then went straight on his way. On his journey, he practiced socializing with the people he meets on his way, and he realizes that not all people are bad. They all wished Drago good luck. Slowly, his social anxiety started to wear off. One night he took a rest in a cave, then he heard a growl echoing deep inside. "Lunas," Drago whispers. Then a fire lit his sword, lighting his way. He finds a dragon deep inside the cave. Its leg injured and is calling for help. He helped the dragon using his healing ability he got from the blood of the phoenix. Then he slowly tamed the dragon. In the morning, he is riding on the dragon's back, slowly getting closer to the land of Vreilla. As he gets closer, the sky is slowly becoming red, and many of the people throw spears at him and his dragon. The dragon lets out a huge fire out his mouth and went straight to the castle where Ragno stays. Ragno is seen at the top of the castle casting lightnings trying to strike the dragon, and Drago. Meanwhile, Drago dodges the attacks, then he jumps off his dragon's back and falls straight in front of Ragno. They both fought each other, casting magic, punching and hitting each other, till Ragno falls on his knees. Ragno didn't think a knight would be this strong and realizes that he is very unlike his ancestors. Ragno looks up at Drago as he kneels, tired from all the fighting. Drago takes off his gauntlet and punches Ragno, turning him into a new man. Then he went inside the castle. He saw the princess. Drago finds her chained and scared. He quickly unchained her, then went back to the kingdom of Elsian.
